About Me

The Artist Behind the Brush

I'm a part-time makeup artist, and honestly, I love it that way. Makeup is my creative escape from the busy everyday life. Keeping it as a side passion makes me genuinely excited every single time someone new sits in my chair. My goal is simple, I just want you to look and feel authentically you, but with an extra touch of radiance.

Whether it is for your wedding day, a special event, or just a simple soft-glam moment, I'm here for it. I bring all my love for makeup into every session, hoping you will leave with a big smile, more confidence, and a little bit of magic.
