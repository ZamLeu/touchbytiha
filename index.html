About Me

The Artist Behind the Brush

I'm a part-time makeup artist — and I wouldn't have it any other way. Makeup is my side passion, my creative outlet between life's other roles. It's that balance that keeps me hungry, humble, and genuinely excited each time a new face sits in my chair — so every look still feels authentically you, only more radiant.

Bridal glow, editorial drama, or an everyday soft-glam moment — whatever you're celebrating, I bring the joy of doing what I love, and leave every face with a little extra confidence, color, and just a bit of magic.
